Privacy Policy

This policy explains how SciPic uses information to provide scientific news, personalized feeds and optional Premium subscriptions.

1. Who we are

SciPic is an independent science-news application operated from the United Arab Emirates under the SciPic name. For privacy questions, requests concerning your personal information or assistance with account deletion, contact scipicapp@gmail.com.

“SciPic” is the name of the application; it does not indicate that SciPic is a separately incorporated company. Further information about the operator's legal identity can be requested through the contact address above, subject to applicable disclosure requirements.

2. Information we process

Guest and registered accounts. SciPic creates a persistent anonymous Supabase account when you first use the app. This involves an account identifier, authentication sessions and technical connection information, which can include an IP address and user agent. If you register with Apple or email, we process the information required for your chosen authentication method. Permanent registration is required before starting a new Premium purchase.

Preferences and saved content. We store your preferred language, time zone, selected science categories, saved article identifiers, collection names and memberships, personalized daily-feed assignments and Free/Premium entitlement status. Article images and authentication material may also be cached on your device.

Subscriptions. We process subscription status and relevant purchase identifiers to provide, validate and restore Premium access. Apple handles payment details; SciPic does not receive your full payment-card number.

Messages. If you email us or report an issue, we process your email address and the information you choose to provide. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless necessary.

Technical data and diagnostics. MWM Spark Flash is initialized when SciPic launches. It processes StoreKit purchase results for purchase-related functions and receives Apple MetricKit performance information. Its complete automatic event and identifier collection has not yet been independently established by SciPic; additional details will be reflected here as authoritative SDK documentation becomes available.

3. Why we use information

  • Provide Free access and personalize your daily science-news feed.
  • Save and synchronize your preferences, favorites and collections across supported sessions and devices.
  • Create and authenticate accounts and provide, validate, restore and manage Premium access.
  • Protect accounts and the service, investigate technical problems and respond to support requests.
  • Assess and improve the app's technical reliability and performance.
  • Meet applicable legal and accounting obligations relating to subscriptions and user requests.

Where applicable privacy law requires a legal basis, processing may rely on providing the service you request, legitimate interests in security and reliability, compliance with legal obligations or consent where required. You can contact us to ask about the basis applicable to a particular use of your information.

4. Service providers and sharing

Supabase. Provides authentication, user-linked database records, content storage and access controls. SciPic's audited production database is hosted in the EU region eu-central-1 (Frankfurt, Germany). Supabase may also process authentication and connection metadata to operate its services.

Apple. Provides Sign in with Apple where selected and StoreKit for in-app subscription purchases, restoration and management. Apple processes payment information under its own terms and privacy practices.

MWM Spark Flash. The SDK is initialized when the app launches, receives StoreKit purchase results for purchase-related functions and sends Apple MetricKit performance metrics to an MWM backend. Its full SDK-specific data categories, automatic events, identifiers and retention practices remain subject to confirmation from MWM. For MWM's published general practices, see the MWM Privacy Policy.

External scientific sources. When you choose to open a linked research or news website, that website may receive your browser request and technical network information under its own privacy policy.

SciPic does not currently display advertising and the previously audited app did not include a separate advertising SDK. This statement should not be interpreted as a verified description of every data practice inside third-party SDKs.

5. Retention

We retain your active account and associated preferences, saved article references, collections and subscription-entitlement records while needed to provide the service, unless you delete your account earlier or applicable law requires otherwise.

SciPic's editorial content and saved-article features are designed around a maximum one-year availability period from publication. Articles and related saved references may be removed earlier if content expires or is withdrawn. This editorial period is not a promise that all authentication, transaction or security records are erased after one year.

When you delete your account, active Supabase account records are removed as described below. Infrastructure backups, security logs and legally required purchase records may follow different retention schedules. We do not currently publish an exact timetable for erasure from every provider backup. Images cached on your device may remain temporarily until cleared by the app or operating system. Contact us for information about a particular record or deletion request.

6. Account deletion

To delete your SciPic account, use the Delete Account option in the app's account settings and follow the confirmation steps. If you cannot access the app, email scipicapp@gmail.com. We may need to verify account ownership before acting on an email request.

Confirmed deletion removes the active Supabase authentication account and associated SciPic profile, preferences, favorites, collections, daily-feed assignments and subscription-entitlement records, subject to lawful retention obligations. Shared scientific articles and public images are editorial content, not individual account records, and are not removed when one user deletes an account.

Provider logs, backups and legally required purchase records may remain under separate retention requirements. MWM-held information is subject to MWM's applicable procedures; contact us if you need assistance identifying the appropriate privacy request route.

Deleting your SciPic account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel an active subscription separately in your device's subscription settings or follow Apple's subscription cancellation instructions. Apple may continue billing until cancellation takes effect.

7. Security and international processing

SciPic uses HTTPS connections and Supabase account-based access policies to restrict access to user records. No service can guarantee absolute security. Although SciPic's audited Supabase production database is hosted in Germany, Apple, MWM and other service providers may process information in other countries. Applicable safeguards and provider practices depend on the service and jurisdiction; consult the relevant providers' privacy policies for their published information.

8. Your choices and rights

You can use SciPic's Free service without permanent registration, although a persistent anonymous technical account is created to operate the service. You can adjust your language and category preferences, register for supported synchronization and Premium purchases, or delete your account through the app.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, a portable copy where applicable, deletion, restriction of or objection to certain processing, or withdrawal of consent where consent is the legal basis. Email scipicapp@gmail.com with your request. We may verify account ownership before disclosing or changing personal information and will respond within the period required by applicable law.

Where applicable, you may also contact a competent data-protection authority. Information held independently by Apple or MWM may be subject to their respective privacy procedures.

9. Children and age-appropriate content

SciPic's scientific news and educational content is intended for readers of all ages. Some subjects, particularly in medicine, biology and other sensitive scientific fields, may contain material better suited to older readers. Understanding the original scientific research articles linked from SciPic may require advanced scientific knowledge or a higher level of education. Parents and guardians are encouraged to guide younger readers where appropriate.

SciPic does not currently verify users' ages. However, this does not remove obligations under applicable children's privacy laws. The app creates a persistent anonymous technical account for each user, and permanent registration or Premium purchases involve additional account and transaction information. Where parental consent or other protections are required, we must comply with those requirements. If we learn that a child's information has been processed without required protections, we will take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

Parents or guardians with questions or concerns about a child's personal information can contact scipicapp@gmail.com. Linked external scientific publishers apply their own content and privacy policies.

10. Updates

We may update this policy as SciPic changes or as additional service-provider information becomes available. The current version and update date will appear on this page. Where required by applicable law, we will provide additional notice or seek consent.
