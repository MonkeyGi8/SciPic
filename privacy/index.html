Privacy Policy

We explain below what information SciPic uses to provide science news, personalized feeds and optional Premium subscriptions.

1. Who we are

SciPic is operated by Stéphane Gigot, United Arab Emirates. For privacy questions or requests, email scipicapp@gmail.com.

2. Information we process

Guest and registered accounts. SciPic creates an anonymous Supabase account when you first use the app. This involves an account identifier, authentication sessions and technical login information, which can include IP address and user agent. When you choose to register using Apple or email, authentication-related account information is processed as required for that method. Permanent registration is required for new Premium purchases.

Preferences and content. We store your preferred language, timezone, selected science categories, saved article identifiers, collection names and memberships, personalized daily-feed assignments, and Free/Premium entitlement status. Some article images and authentication material are cached on your device.

Subscriptions. We process subscription status and relevant purchase identifiers to provide and restore Premium access. Apple handles payment details; SciPic does not collect payment-card numbers.

Messages. If you contact us or report an error, we process your email address and the information you choose to provide. Please do not include sensitive personal information unnecessarily.

Diagnostics and purchase validation. MWM Spark Flash receives Apple performance metrics and processes StoreKit purchase results used for purchase validation and service operation.

3. Why we use information

  • Provide the Free service and a personalized daily science-news feed.
  • Save and synchronize your preferences, articles and collections across supported sessions and devices.
  • Create and authenticate accounts and manage Premium purchases, restoration and access.
  • Protect accounts and the service, investigate technical problems and respond to support requests.
  • Measure and improve app reliability and performance.

Where applicable privacy law requires a specific legal basis, this may include providing the requested service, legitimate interests in security and reliability, legal obligations, and consent where required.

4. Service providers and sharing

Supabase. Provides authentication, user-linked database records, content storage and access control. The audited production database is hosted in the EU region eu-central-1 (Frankfurt, Germany). Supabase may process authentication and connection metadata as part of operating the service.

Apple. Provides Sign in with Apple where selected and StoreKit for in-app subscription purchases, restoration and management. Apple processes payment details under its own terms.

MWM Spark Flash. The SDK is initialized when the app launches, receives StoreKit purchase results for validation, and sends Apple MetricKit performance metrics to an MWM backend. MWM states that it processes technical and usage information to operate, secure and improve its services, and may process data through service providers and international transfers subject to applicable safeguards. For details, see MWM Privacy Policy.

External scientific sources. If you open a linked research or news website, that site may receive your browser request and network metadata under its own privacy policy.

SciPic does not currently display advertising and the audited app had no separate advertising SDK.

5. Retention

We keep account-linked information while needed to provide your account, preferences, Saved SciPics and subscription access, unless it is deleted earlier or applicable law requires otherwise.

Scientific articles and associated images are subject to separate editorial expiration and cleanup rules; article removal may also remove linked favorites and feed entries. On-device image caches may remain until cleared by the app or operating system. Provider logs, transaction records and backups may follow separate retention schedules under the applicable providers' policies and legal obligations.

6. Account deletion

SciPic offers account creation and in-app account deletion. To delete your account, use the Delete Account option in the app's account settings or contact scipicapp@gmail.com if you cannot access it. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

Deleting your account removes the Supabase authentication account and associated SciPic profile, preferences, favorites, collections, feed assignments and entitlement records, subject to any lawful retention obligations. Shared scientific articles and public assets are not personal account records. Third-party logs, backups and certain purchase records may persist under provider or legal retention requirements. MWM processes data used by its services under its own privacy policy. Requests concerning MWM-held personal data may be handled in accordance with MWM's published privacy procedures.

Deleting your SciPic account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. Manage or cancel your subscription through Apple's subscription settings before deleting your account.

7. Security and international processing

SciPic uses HTTPS connections and Supabase account-based access policies to restrict access to user records. No service can guarantee absolute security. Although SciPic's audited Supabase database is hosted in Germany, Apple, MWM and other service providers may process information in other countries. MWM states that it may process personal data internationally and relies on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

8. Your choices and rights

You can use SciPic as a guest for Free access, adjust your language and category preferences, register to synchronize content, and request account deletion. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your information, restrict or object to processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Contact scipicapp@gmail.com to exercise your rights. You may also have the right to contact a competent data-protection authority.

9. Children

SciPic is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to SciPic, please contact us so that we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

10. Updates

We may update this policy as SciPic changes or when additional provider information becomes available. The latest version and its update date will appear at this page. Where required, we will provide additional notice or seek consent.
