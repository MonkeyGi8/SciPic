2. Subscriptions

SciPic offers an optional Premium subscription through Apple's App Store.

Subscription payments, renewals and cancellations are managed through your Apple ID and the App Store.

You can view or manage your active subscriptions from your Apple account subscription settings.

Cancelling your SciPic account does not automatically cancel an active Apple subscription.

For instructions on managing or cancelling an Apple subscription, see Apple's subscription cancellation instructions .

3. Restore Purchases

If you reinstall SciPic, change device, or need to recover an eligible Premium subscription, use the Restore Purchases option available in SciPic.

Premium access is linked to a SciPic account. Make sure you are signed in to the appropriate account when restoring Premium access.

If your subscription cannot be restored, contact us at scipicapp@gmail.com .

4. Account and App Data

SciPic can be used with Free access without permanent registration. A persistent anonymous technical account is created to operate the service.

Permanent registration is required before starting a new Premium purchase.

If you need assistance with your SciPic account, saved SciPics, collections, Premium status, or other account-related information, contact SciPic Support.

To delete a registered SciPic account, use the Delete Account option available in the app's account settings and follow the confirmation steps.

If you cannot access the app, contact scipicapp@gmail.com .

5. Report an Error

SciPic aims to present scientific news clearly and accurately. If you notice an incorrect story, source, translation, image, scientific statement, or technical problem, please let us know.

When possible, include:

  • The title of the SciPic concerned.
  • A short description of the issue.
  • A screenshot if it helps explain the problem.

Send your report to scipicapp@gmail.com .

6. About SciPic

SciPic transforms scientific news and research into striking visuals and concise explanations, helping readers understand important scientific developments at a glance.

Each SciPic can include a summary, why the research matters, limits and uncertainties, a confidence indicator, publication information and links to underlying sources.

SciPic covers fields including space and astronomy, physics, biology, health and medicine, Earth and climate, technology and AI, archaeology and history, and chemistry and materials.

Important
SciPic provides scientific news and summaries for informational and educational purposes only. Health and medical content does not constitute medical advice and should not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
