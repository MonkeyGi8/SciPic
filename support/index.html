Subscriptions

SciPic Premium subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID and the App Store. You can view, change, or cancel a subscription from your Apple account subscription settings.

If you change device or reinstall SciPic, use the Restore Purchases option in the app to restore an eligible Premium subscription.

Account and app data

If you need assistance with your SciPic account, saved SciPics, collections, or account deletion, contact us using the email address above.

Report an error

If you notice an incorrect story, source, translation, image, or technical problem, please email us with a short description. When useful, include the SciPic title and a screenshot so we can investigate.

About SciPic

SciPic presents science news in a visual and accessible format, combining an image with concise summaries and links to sources.

SciPic content is provided for informational and educational purposes only. Health and medical content is not medical advice and should not replace consultation with a qualified healthcare professional.
